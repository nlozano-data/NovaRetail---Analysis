## 📌 Project Overview

How does customer behavior relate to the revenue generated by an e-commerce business?

This project explores customer behavior data from **NovaRetail+**, a Latin American e-commerce platform, to identify the factors most strongly associated with annual customer revenue.

Using Python, statistical analysis, and data visualization, this study examines purchasing frequency, platform engagement, advertising expenditure, customer satisfaction, premium membership, and customer churn.

The ultimate goal is to transform raw data into meaningful business insights that can support strategic decisions around customer acquisition, conversion, retention, and revenue growth.

**Main Business Question:**

Which customer behavior variables are most strongly associated with annual revenue generation?

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Explore and understand customer behavior through descriptive statistics.
- Clean and prepare the dataset for statistical analysis.
- Identify relationships between numerical, binary, and categorical variables.
- Measure correlations using appropriate statistical methods.
- Visualize key behavioral patterns and relationships.
- Translate statistical findings into actionable business recommendations.

## 📊 Dataset Description

The analysis uses a dataset containing:

| Feature | Description |
|---|---|
| Customer ID | Unique customer identifier |
| Age | Customer age |
| Income Level | Estimated annual customer income |
| Monthly Visits | Number of monthly platform visits |
| Monthly Purchases | Number of purchases per month |
| Targeted Advertising Spend | Advertising expenditure assigned to the customer |
| Satisfaction | Customer satisfaction score (1–5) |
| Premium Membership | Binary membership indicator |
| Churn | Binary customer abandonment indicator |
| Device Type | Mobile, desktop, or tablet |
| Region | Customer geographical region |
| Annual Revenue | Annual revenue generated by the customer |

**Dataset size:** 15,000 customer records and 12 variables.

**Data quality:** The initial exploration identified no missing values across the dataset.

**Main analytical metric:** `ingreso_anual`.

## 🔍 Analytical Methodology

The project follows a structured data analysis workflow:

**1. Data Exploration**
- Dataset inspection and structural validation.
- Identification of data types and missing values.
- Descriptive statistics and distribution analysis.

**2. Data Cleaning & Preparation**
- Validation of variable types.
- Conversion of customer age from float to integer.
- Identification of numerical, binary, and categorical variables.
- Documentation of analytical assumptions.

**3. Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)**
- Correlation heatmap.
- Scatterplots for selected variable pairs.
- Identification of patterns, dispersion, and potential outliers.

**4. Statistical Analysis**

Different statistical methods were selected according to variable types:

- **Pearson Correlation:** Linear relationships between numerical variables.
- **Spearman Correlation:** Monotonic relationships between numerical variables.
- **Point-Biserial Correlation:** Relationships between binary and numerical variables.
- **Cramér's V:** Associations between categorical variables.

**5. Business Interpretation**
- Identification of key findings.
- Assessment of statistical significance and practical relevance.
- Business implications and strategic recommendations.

## 📈 Key Findings

### 1. Purchase Frequency: The Strongest Association with Annual Revenue

The relationship between monthly purchases and annual revenue was the strongest observed in the analysis.

| Statistical Method | Correlation |
|---|---:|
| Pearson | 0.967 |
| Spearman | 0.967 |

This indicates an exceptionally strong positive association.

**Business insight:** Purchase frequency is a key behavioral indicator associated with customer revenue. Further investigation into purchasing patterns, repeat purchases, and customer loyalty may help identify opportunities for business growth.

**Important consideration:** The methodology used to calculate annual revenue must be validated to rule out a direct mathematical dependency between purchases and revenue.

### 2. Platform Engagement Shows a Positive Association with Revenue

Monthly visits show a positive association with annual revenue and monthly purchases.

| Relationship | Pearson | Spearman |
|---|---:|---:|
| Visits vs. Annual Revenue | 0.340 | ≈ 0.340 |
| Visits vs. Monthly Purchases | 0.354 | 0.333 |
| Visits vs. Advertising Spend | 0.579 | 0.559 |

**Business insight:** Increasing platform engagement may represent an opportunity to improve the customer journey and identify conversion opportunities.

The next step should be to investigate how browsing activity translates into purchases.

### 3. Customer Segmentation Variables Show Weak Associations

The point-biserial analysis identified a weak positive association between premium membership and annual revenue.

| Relationship | Correlation | p-value |
|---|---:|---:|
| Premium Membership vs. Revenue | 0.0931 | 3.09 × 10⁻³⁰ |
| Churn vs. Revenue | -0.0028 | 0.7295 |

Although the premium membership relationship is statistically significant, its effect size is small.

The analysis of categorical variables using Cramér's V also identified practically negligible associations:

- Region vs. Device Type: 0.0124
- Region vs. Premium Membership: 0.0126
- Device Type vs. Premium Membership: 0.0197

**Business insight:** Customer segmentation strategies should consider behavioral and economic indicators instead of relying exclusively on geographical or technological characteristics.

## 💡 Strategic Business Recommendations

Based on the analytical findings, the following opportunities are proposed:

1. **Customer Retention & Loyalty:** Identify high-frequency purchasers and develop strategies to encourage repeat purchases and strengthen customer relationships.

2. **Conversion Optimization:** Analyze the customer journey to understand how platform visits translate into purchasing decisions.

3. **Premium Membership Evaluation:** Review the value proposition of the premium program and investigate differences between subscribers and non-subscribers.

4. **Advanced Customer Segmentation:** Classify customers according to purchase frequency, engagement, and revenue contribution.

5. **Further Statistical Validation:** Investigate the relationship between purchase frequency and annual revenue before using it for financial projections or predictive models.

6. **Experimentation:** Design A/B tests to evaluate loyalty incentives, conversion strategies, and customer engagement initiatives.

## ⚠️ Limitations

This project is exploratory and correlational.

- Correlation does not imply causation.
- The dataset represents customer behavior during 2024.
- The strong relationship between monthly purchases and annual revenue may involve mathematical dependency.
- Other relevant variables, such as discounts, product categories, prices, and seasonality, are not included.
- Statistical significance does not necessarily imply practical business significance.
- Additional analyses are required to validate the observed patterns.
